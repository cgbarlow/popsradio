# Grandad's Radio Platform Image Prompts

Nov 15, 2026 · @Chris Barlow

Seven prompts for an image generator, for the platform unit in [Grandad's Radio as a Platform](Grandad's%20Radio%20as%20a%20Platform.md): the bedside scene redone with the new slim radio, a blueprint, a chess clock in a rest home, Pong from the player's seat, FM radio on the screen, the Talk list on the screen, and the radio carried with its strap over a shoulder.

## How to use

Paste one prompt at a time. Each is self-contained, so the generator needs no other context.

- **Consistency.** Every prompt repeats the same description of the radio (the paragraph that starts "A slim upright tabletop radio"), so the results look like one product. Change it in all seven if you change it in one.
- **The depth.** The radio is a slim slab: about 160 mm wide, 150 mm tall and 50 mm deep. Generators drift back towards a cube. If one does, add "very slim, seen from the side it is less than a third as deep as it is tall".
- **The strap.** A long, adjustable shoulder strap with a slide buckle, not a short handle. Generators default to a short handle arching over the top. On a table, the prompts lay the spare length in loose loops beside the radio so its length shows.
- **The orange button is the biggest thing on top.** It is about 40 mm wide and domed. The volume knob beside it is small, about 25 mm. Generators tend to make the knob big and the button small; if that happens, add "the orange button is twice the size of the volume knob".
- **The knobs.** Three black ribbed knobs: a small volume knob on top to the right of the orange button, and a big one sticking out of each side. Generators like to add more knobs or put them on the front. If one does, add "only three knobs in total" to the end.
- **One light.** One green status light, to the left of the screen. Generators like to add a matching one on the other side.
- **On its back.** In prompts 3 and 4 the radio lies on its back with the screen facing up. The top edge, with the orange button, points away from the player, and the speaker grille is nearest them.
- **Faces.** Prompts 3, 4 and 7 keep faces out of frame. If the generator adds one anyway, add "cropped at the chest, no faces visible".
- **Screen text.** Each prompt spells out every word on the screen and ends with "no other text on the screen", because generators fill an empty screen with made-up station names. If the words come out wrong, cut the small text first.
- **No antenna.** The FM antenna is a wire hidden inside the strap, so the prompts say there is none. Generators love adding one.
- **Aspect ratio.** 4:5 for most, 16:9 for the blueprint, 3:2 for the chess game. Change the last line if your tool sets this elsewhere.

In prompt 3, swap the clock times for a pinball table, with one hand pressing each side knob as flippers. Add "exploded view showing the circuit board, speaker, battery and antenna wire floating apart above the case" to prompt 2 for a cutaway.
