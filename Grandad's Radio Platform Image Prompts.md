# Grandad's Radio Platform Image Prompts

Nov 15, 2026 · @Chris Barlow

Seven prompts for an image generator, for the platform unit in [Grandad's Radio as a Platform](Grandad's%20Radio%20as%20a%20Platform.md): the bedside scene redone with the new box, a blueprint, a chess clock in a rest home, Pong from the player's seat, FM radio on the screen, the Talk list on the screen, and the radio carried under an arm.

## How to use

Paste one prompt at a time. Each is self-contained, so the generator needs no other context.

- **Consistency.** Every prompt repeats the same description of the radio (the paragraph that starts "A boxy tabletop radio"), so the results look like one product. Change it in all seven if you change it in one.
- **The knobs.** Three big black ribbed knobs: one on top to the right of the orange button, one sticking out of each side. Generators like to add more knobs or put them on the front. If one does, add "only three knobs in total" to the end.
- **On its back.** In prompts 3 and 4 the radio lies on its back with the screen facing up. The top edge, with the orange button and strap, points away from the player, and the speaker grille is nearest them.
- **Faces.** Prompts 3, 4 and 7 keep faces out of frame. If the generator adds one anyway, add "cropped at the chest, no faces visible".
- **Screen text.** Generators often garble small text. The screens use a few large words and numbers. If they come out wrong, cut the small text first.
- **No antenna.** The FM antenna is a wire hidden inside the strap, so the prompts say there is none. Generators love adding one.
- **Aspect ratio.** 4:5 for most, 16:9 for the blueprint, 3:2 for the chess game. Change the last line if your tool sets this elsewhere.
